# Token Survivor

A fast-paced satirical browser survival game where players dodge AI corporate rate-limits, safety censorship, and silent downgrades while hoarding leaked API keys and open weights to keep their context window alive.

## Language

### Player & Resources

**Context Window**:
The player's remaining lifespan and stamina, which drains constantly and depletes heavily upon taking damage.
_Avoid_: Health, HP, Mana, Life

**Open Weights**:
A collectible item that restores a substantial portion of the player's Context Window.
_Avoid_: Health pack, Potion, Heart

**Leaked API Key**:
A rare collectible that grants temporary invulnerability, speed boost, and a resource magnet.
_Avoid_: Power-up, Star, Super mode

**Prompt Node**:
The player avatar navigating the rate-limit arena.
_Avoid_: Character, Ship, Player, Avatar

### Hazards & Corporate Enemies

**429 Boulder**:
A heavy falling projectile representing corporate rate limits that deals major damage and knocks the Prompt Node back.
_Avoid_: Obstacle, Rock, Hazard

**Silent Nerf Beam**:
A fast, narrow projectile that slows down Prompt Node movement and chips away at the Context Window.
_Avoid_: Laser, Slow debuff, Bullet

**Safety Refusal Wall**:
A wide, drifting blockade inscribed with preachy AI apology disclaimers that forces the player to maneuver around it.
_Avoid_: Shield, Barrier, Censorship wall

**Corporate Reset Timer**:
An escalating arena countdown that accelerates hazard spawn frequency and shrinks play area boundaries.
_Avoid_: Wave timer, Difficulty ramp

### Sharing & Metagame

**Post-Mortem Card**:
A sharable summary card containing survival duration, tokens burned, cause of death, and a seed-challenge link.
_Avoid_: Scoreboard, Game over screen, Result page

**Challenge Seed**:
A deterministic seed encoded in the URL hash that reproduces the exact hazard timeline for peer challenges.
_Avoid_: Level ID, Room code, Game parameter
